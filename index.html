Prove it prototype

Each level is a mirror puzzle whose best solution is known. You don't solve it: you prove that nothing shorter exists. Claim that a mirror must flip or must stay. A claim is accepted only if the opposite would leave some target with no light at all, even though every mirror you haven't claimed may send light both ways. Prove as many forced flips as the best solution has, and no solution can be shorter.

Solid light is certain. Dashed light is light that might get there, given your claims. Settled mirrors have a dot: blue, proven to flip; white, proven to stay. Unsettled mirrors show their starting tilt over a faint X. Keys: arrows, Enter, F/S to pick the claim, U to undo.
