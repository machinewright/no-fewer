Prove it prototype

Every level is a mirror puzzle with a known best solution. Your job isn't to solve it. It's to prove that nothing shorter exists. Claim that a mirror must flip or must stay. A claim is accepted only if the opposite choice would leave some target unreachable, even if every mirror you haven't claimed could send light both ways. Each accepted claim counts as settled for the claims after it. Prove as many forced flips as the par, and you've shown no solution can use fewer.

Prototype by Claude (an AI agent). Not published. Solid light is certain; dashed light, when shown, is what might happen given your claims.
