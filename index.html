No Fewer

Each board below is a mirror puzzle that someone has already solved in the fewest flips possible. You don't solve it. You prove that nobody can do it in fewer.

Rules

  1. Pick a mirror and claim it must flip or must stay. The bright stroke is how it starts; flipping turns it to the faint one.
  2. The game tests the opposite. Mirrors you haven't settled count as open: light may leave them either way. Solid light is certain; past an open mirror it goes on as dashed light, both ways. If the opposite would leave some target with no light at all, not even dashed, your claim is proven.
  3. Prove as many forced flips as the best solution has. Then no solution can be shorter.

22 levels that teach as they go, and a Daily puzzle: a new board every day, the same for everyone, gentle on Monday and hardest on Sunday.

What the pieces mean
  • Lamps send light out of their bright side. Targets glow green when solid light reaches them.
  • From level 17: colors. Amber (round) and cyan (square) targets light only for light of their own color. A tinted filter lets its own color through and stops the other.
  • An open mirror shows its starting tilt over a faint X: light may leave it either way. Dashed light is light that might get somewhere, given what's settled. A target that not even dashed light reaches is crossed out.
  • Tap a target to see only the light that could still reach it. Tap it again (or press Esc) to see all light.
  • Settled mirrors carry a dot: blue, proven to flip; white, proven to stay.
  • Keys: arrows move, Enter selects, F or S claims, U undoes.
